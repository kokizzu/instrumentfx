### GE250 category-choice evidence

The original manufacturer's appendix documents **33 shared FXA/FXB choices**, **24 DS/OD choices**, **70 factory amp choices**, **32 factory cabinets**, and **9 delay choices**. Shared FXA/FXB algorithms are not counted twice as66 unique effects. Empty user import slots are not factory algorithms. Complete noise/EQ/reverb subtotals remain untranscribed. [M40,M41,M43,M47,M49,M51]

The official global page advertises70 amps and180+ effects. The Japanese page retains55-amp/152-effect/28-cab language alongside32-cab and180+ figures. These conflicting generations/inclusion conventions remain visible; no invented definitive grand total is calculated. [G,J]

### Connections, recording and stage functions

One6.35 mm mono instrument input; two6.35 mm mono main outs plus two balanced XLR outs; dedicated3.5 mm stereo AUX IN and headphones; mono6.35 mm FX send/return; one external EXP2; USB-B; **one five-pin MIDI socket configurable as IN or OUT**, not separate physical IN and OUT ports; DC. XLR outputs do not establish a microphone preamp or phantom power. [M8,M36,J]

USB direct recording and reamping with dry/wet routing are documented; internal24-bit/44.1 kHz processing. Exact USB input/output channel count was not numerically established in the inspected primary material and remains unknown. [M34,M35,J]

**70-second looper**, PRE/POST placement,60 drum patterns plus10 metronomes, tuner,CTRL switch assignments,delay/reverb trails and separate global-EQ routing are documented. The Japanese introductory150-second looper sentence contradicts its own70-second feature list and the global description;70 is retained with that conflict disclosed, not silently upgraded to150. [G,J]

Tone Capture and four-cable use involve instrument/line-level connections and the amplifier's FX loop. An amplifier speaker output must not be connected directly to the processor's signal input. [M19]

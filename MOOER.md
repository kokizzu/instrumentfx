### GE250 category-choice evidence

The original manufacturer's appendix documents **33 shared FXA/FXB choices**, **24 DS/OD choices**, **70 factory amp choices**, **32 factory cabinets**, and **9 delay choices**. Shared FXA/FXB algorithms are not counted twice as66 unique effects. Empty user import slots are not factory algorithms. Complete noise/EQ/reverb subtotals remain untranscribed. [M40,M41,M43,M47,M49,M51]

The official global page advertises70 amps and180+ effects. The Japanese page retains55-amp/152-effect/28-cab language alongside32-cab and180+ figures. These conflicting generations/inclusion conventions remain visible; no invented definitive grand total is calculated. [G,J]

### Connections, recording and stage functions

One6.35 mm mono instrument input; two6.35 mm mono main outs plus two balanced XLR outs; dedicated3.5 mm stereo AUX IN and headphones; mono6.35 mm FX send/return; one external EXP2; USB-B; **one five-pin MIDI socket configurable as IN or OUT**, not separate physical IN and OUT ports; DC. XLR outputs do not establish a microphone preamp or phantom power. [M8,M36,J]

USB direct recording and reamping with dry/wet routing are documented; internal24-bit/44.1 kHz processing. Exact USB input/output channel count was not numerically established in the inspected primary material and remains unknown. [M34,M35,J]

**70-second looper**, PRE/POST placement,60 drum patterns plus10 metronomes, tuner,CTRL switch assignments,delay/reverb trails and separate global-EQ routing are documented. The Japanese introductory150-second looper sentence contradicts its own70-second feature list and the global description;70 is retained with that conflict disclosed, not silently upgraded to150. [G,J]

Tone Capture and four-cable use involve instrument/line-level connections and the amplifier's FX loop. An amplifier speaker output must not be connected directly to the processor's signal input. [M19]

## GE300

**Checked 2026-10-16; partial exact-model profile.** Original GE300, not GE300 Lite. Original manufacturer manual (2019 document, V1.1.0 screen examples) and current model-specific v5.1.4 notes are kept separate. Guitar multi-effects including polyphonic synth; bass suitability remains unclassified pending explicit exact-model evidence. [G][M][F]

### GE300 — processing and memory

| Field | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Processing blocks |15 category blocks, CPU-limited, movable except input-side SYNTH; two FX categories do not make every category freely duplicable |
| Preset memory |256 editable total memories; current factory-filled/user-reserved split unknown |
| Synth / acoustic |Three-voice polyphonic synth without special pickup; AMP106–108 explicitly acoustic simulators. Tone Capture instrument matching is separate |

**15 category blocks**, each with a dedicated button: SYNTH,COMP,WAH,FXA,DS/OD,AMP,CAB,NS,TONE CAP,EQ,FXB,FX LOOP,DELAY,REVERB,VOL. These are not 15 freely duplicable slots; CPU load can limit combinations. Blocks and output/send/return tap positions are movable, but SYNTH has a fixed input-side position with parallel voice routing; later notes prohibit RETURN before SYNTH. Stereo and higher-quality reverb consume more CPU. [M][F]

The old **317 total types /164 effects /108 amps /43 cabinets** are historical manufacturer counts, not a verified current v5.1.4 inventory. Subsequent releases add harmony,vibe,drives,FX and ambience models; a complete reconciled current per-category count remains unknown. [M][G][F]

Exact sub-patch count and seamless switching/tail guarantees remain unverified. [M][F]

### GE300 — IR, NAM and software

Original **20 user IR slots** became **50**, with **80 MNRS slots** documented in v4.0.1. v5.1.0 supports two simultaneous1024-point IRs in DUAL CAB. Original2048-point support and the specification table's literal2018 typo are retained as a source conflict. WAV24bit44.1kHz is listed originally; later32-bit import fix exists, but full current import conversion/rate rules remain unresolved. [M][F]

**NAM A1/A2 through conversion**, AMP module only, v5.1.4 (2026-08-04); not native NAM execution. Converter fidelity, architecture subvariants and current concurrent model limits remain unknown. MOOER .GNR/.GIR imports and onboard Tone Capture are proprietary paths, not interchangeable format claims. [F]

**MOOER Studio editor for Windows and macOS**: parameter editing,preset/IR transfer,backup and updates. Current exact OS minimums unknown; old product lists Win7/8/10 and macOS10.10–10.15, not a current support promise. Windows USB ASIO driver is separate from the editor; original Mac USB audio is plug-and-play. Mobile editing remains unknown. No software was installed. [M][G][F]

### GE300 — complete ports and live features

**I/O:** 6.35mm mono instrument input with guitar/line selection; 3.5mm stereo AUX; two6.35mm L/R outputs; two balanced XLR outputs with ground lift; 6.35mm stereo headphones; stereo FX loop with two send and two return TS jacks; EXP2 TRS expression/amp-control; five-pin DIN MIDI IN and OUT/THRU; USB-B; DC input and power switch. XLR sockets are outputs: no physical microphone input or phantom supply documented in the complete rear inventory. [M]

**USB audio2-in/2-out,24bit44.1kHz**, selectable dry/effected recording,monitor mix and reamp. Distinguish hardware MIDI sockets from unverified USB MIDI capability. Output tap routing supports simultaneous PA cab simulation and an amplifier path without cab; four-cable rigs and stereo external processing are documented. [M][G]

**30-minute stereo looper**, overdub,undo/redo,reverse,half-speed/one-shot and saved loop import/export/backup. Tuner and tap tempo; built-in drum/rhythm support unknown. Original manual has two footswitch modes; later firmware adds a third. Programmable controls do not establish a specific scene ceiling. [M][F]

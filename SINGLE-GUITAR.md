Dedicated synthesis, acoustic simulation, vocoding and pitch pedals are kept here, **outside ALL-GUITAR and active multi-effects counts**. This is preserved partial research checked 2026-10-14, reorganized 2026-10-16, not a new price/specification verification. A mode or preset count is not a simultaneous multi-effects architecture.

## Excluded archive — not part of the multi-effects comparison

The five records below preserve previously collected evidence only. **Do not restore them to active candidate tables or counts on resume.** Multiple modes, presets or related sounds do not automatically turn a dedicated-purpose pedal into a general guitar multi-effects processor. See [SCOPE.md](README.md#scope-and-ownership)<!-- historical-link:single-guitar-001 -->.

K/F/B means independently adjustable rotary controls / main footswitches / separate buttons. Concentric controls are explicit; toe and power/mode switches are additional. | Excluded model | Architecture and choices | Memory | Function / loading |

| SY-200 | One selected polyphonic synth engine; **171 sounds across 12 categories**; individual category counts not transcribed | **128 editable memories**; factory-filled subset ? | Guitar/bass synth, standard input; micro-USB documented for updates, not IR/NAM import |

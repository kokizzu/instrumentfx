**Checked through 2026-11-21. 4/402 active models have firmware audits; 3/402 have pitch audits; 0 supplemental/archival records are separate from active counts.** Each model is updated individually. Audited records can retain unresolved fields; this is not a claim that every specification is verified.

Polyphonic here concerns simultaneous input notes in the identified pitch algorithm. Stereo, two harmony voices, octave effects, synth polyphony and vocal pitch correction do not by themselves prove whole-chord transposition. Preset recall and the exact firmware/algorithm remain separate checks.

DSP percentages require the measurement basis, processor/path denominator, firmware and effect-chain configuration. Published specifications and owner measurements remain labelled separately. A fixed block count is not a CPU percentage, and unpublished resource costs are not zero. Values across unlike processors or denominators are not directly comparable.

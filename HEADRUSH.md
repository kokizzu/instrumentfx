### MX5 category choices

The current product-page list includes the three effects added in firmware **2.7**, released **2024-08-08**. Counts are listed algorithms, not simultaneous engines: **52 amps; 15 cabinets; 10 microphone models; Distortion 17; Dynamics/EQ 11; Modulation 22; Reverb/Delay 13; Expression 11**. The five effect groups sum to **74**; cabinets, microphones, IR files and the FX-loop utility are separate. [P], [U]

### Connections and stage operation

One 6.35 mm mono guitar input; stereo TRS send and return; two balanced 6.35 mm main outputs; separate 3.5 mm headphones and aux; separate 3.5 mm MIDI IN/OUT via supplied DIN adapters; external EXP; USB-B; DC. No XLR/microphone/phantom connection in the published inventory. [M]

Scenes, setlists and hands-free editing are documented. Looper: up to **50 layers, five minutes per layer, 20 minutes combined**, one loop at a time. [F] **Spillover caveat:** the manual says between-rig tails do not work while using FX Loop or USB Audio; do not repeat the unqualified marketing promise. [M, p.10] Firmware 2.7 is the version exposed by the retrieved download index, not proof an owner's unit has been updated. [D]

## Scope, prices and organization

No fresh hardware specification is inferred solely from a brand or product name. Refer to each complete record for screen size/color/touch,physical controls,blocks/category counts,I/O,presets,power,IR/NAM,apps and separate used/new IDR. Sold-out or indexed prices are not checkout stock; a factory cabinet library is not proof of third-party IR import.

Legacy links are temporarily retained because their full information has not yet been merged into root brand files. The root layout is **not completely migrated**. New findings must go to root `[BRAND].md`; continuation and unresolved classification stay in `research-state.json`,not a new dated/progress Markdown file. No GitHub workflow or automation is used. Former/donated eligible owner units remain in the catalogue without exposing private purchase data.

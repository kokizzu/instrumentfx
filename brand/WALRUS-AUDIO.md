## ACS1 MKII — partial no-treadle discovery

Checked **2026-11-03**. Separate from original ACS1. **Outside active counts** pending scope/profile and exact Indonesian hardware verification. Manufacturer-hosted 11-page exact-model manual, linked from its product page; original text, not generated image descriptions.

| Field | Evidence / qualification |
|---|---|
| Scope | Amp/cab processor plus gate, EQ and three reverbs: Room, Hall, Spring. Broader multi-effects admission remains under review. [Product][WA-P] |
| Expression | No analog EXP socket in the manual connector inventory; no built-in treadle. MIDI CC volume is separate. Direct analog global-volume requirement fails this documented connection path. [Manual pp3,7–8,11][WA-M] |
| Headphones / navigation | Dedicated phones; Volume controls it. Both switches cycle three presets within the selected bank; not a dedicated down/up pair. 128 total slots, not 128 plus three. [Manual pp3,6–7][WA-M] |
| Controls / display | Text names six rotaries including three encoders, two footswitches; separate-button/visual census pending. Product specifies shared 2in OLED,128×32; not individual switch screens. [WA-P][WA-M] |
| Footprint conflict | Manual p11:74.33W×124.37D×63.15H mm including knobs/jacks, preferred rectangle fits. Product instead gives4.9×2.52×2.64in without axes. Preserve conflict; no unconditional fit certification. Manual mass0.8lb. |
| Ports / power | Two TS inputs/two TS outputs, phones, MIDI IN/THRU, USB-C,9VDC center-negative,300mA minimum. USB documented for IR/firmware, not proven audio or bus power. [WA-M] |
| Processing / loading | Six amps,twelve stock IRs; independent L/R amp/cab choices. User IR loading documented. Native/converted NAM and capture unsupported by current evidence. [WA-P] |
| IDR | Exact Shopee search found no qualified item. Condition,stock,variant,bundle,voucher,region and used/new prices unknown; not evidence of absence. |

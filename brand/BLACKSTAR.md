### ID:X Floor Two — patch pickup, tuner and MIDI scope

Checked **2026-11-06**, same [256401M-VA-1 01/26 family manual](https://www.kirstein.de/docs/manuals/00118929/manual_EN.pdf?h=2b60f8933d3a40a7df062be52bf93b84), printed23–26. Patch recall loads saved amp values immediately; physical knobs take over after crossing the displayed saved-value tick. Manual mode follows physical knob positions. This is knob pickup, not expression-position recall.

Hold TAP for the chromatic tuner; any button/control exits. Tuner mutes audio by default, configurable in Architect; detection range/reference calibration remain unverified. MIDI PC0 selects Manual, PC1–99 selects the99 patches, and PC100–127 is ignored; CC32 also selects0–99. Separate CC101/102 control reverb/delay tails; CC103/104 trigger their separate taps at127, and CC105 opens/closes tuner. CabRig GLOBAL/PATCH commands CC111/112 (values≥64) address CabRig, not global volume assignment. Channel configuration and expression-volume MIDI mapping remain unverified. No commands were sent to hardware.

## G3S — controller discovery lead

Checked **2026-11-01** against the [manufacturer G3S page](https://www.thegigrig.com/g3-s). Partial controller/switcher record, outside active multi-effects counts. Original G3 and Atom S are separate models.

| Field | Evidence / qualification |
|---|---|
| Display layout | **Twelve dedicated OLED preset screens plus one main TFT LCD**; thirteen physical displays provide labels for fourteen switches. Detailed feature list calls the small screens OLED; Quicktech instead groups all thirteen under TFT LCD. Preserve this internal wording conflict; do not classify the footswitch screens as confirmed LCDs. |
| Display dimensions | Main **36×48 mm**; small screens **7×25 mm**, with adjustable brightness and up to five characters. Two-axis measurements, not diagonals. Color and touch remain unverified. |
| Hardware role | **Twelve passive effects loops**, pairable as six stereo loops; external pedals provide the effects. Programmable pre/post gain does not establish a global analog expression-volume role. |
| Memory / MIDI | Up to **99 banks ×28 presets**; up to twenty PC/CC messages per preset and two expression inputs for CC control. Factory occupancy, pedal wiring and endpoints unknown. |
| Body / power | **440 W×130 D mm**, rear height78 mm/front55 mm including switches; **2 kg**. Published **9 V DC,2.1 mm center-negative,950 mA**; rating is not our measured draw. Width exceeds the preferred footprint. |

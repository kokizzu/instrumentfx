### Creative processing and bypass-routing qualifications

Checked **2026-11-05**. The current manufacturer product page [P] identifies HOWL as resonant-filter fuzz, SWELL as envelope-controlled volume, and PITCH as shifting **−1 to +1 octave**. REVERSE changes delay playback speed/pitch over that octave range; COLLAGE combines delay, granular loops, pitch bends and glitches. These descriptions establish neither a dedicated tracking synth nor input-note polyphony. FILTER offers low-pass, high-pass and tilt alternatives, not three simultaneous filters.

The original manufacturer-authored **firmware1.04,50-page manual**, readable on an [Equipboard mirror](https://images.equipboard.com/uploads/item/manual/114096/hologram-electronics-chroma-console-manual.pdf), matches the previously recorded cover revision and section structure. Printed pp41,44 distinguish buffered bypass (with or without trails) from relay true bypass: **automatic mono-to-stereo/stereo-to-mono routing does not apply while true-bypassed**; each output connects to its corresponding input. Buffered trails preserve DIFFUSION decay without accepting new audio. This does not establish expression-volume attenuation of tails or loops.

Printed p44 specifies **maximum input +8dBu;24-bit/48kHz conversion;1MΩ input;output impedance <1kΩ**. These ratings do not establish USB audio, a headphone amplifier or measured latency. No current-device or installed-firmware test was performed. Existing expression, physical-display, dimension-axis, mass and Indonesia-offer gaps remain open.

### Capture audio versus saved Gesture automation

Checked **2026-11-05** in the [manufacturer-authored firmware1.04 manual,printed pp24–26,31](https://images.equipboard.com/uploads/item/manual/114096/hologram-electronics-chroma-console-manual.pdf). Presets retain Gesture recordings and Capture routing, **not Capture audio**. Holding TAP records; release starts playback. Recording again replaces the previous phrase; one TAP stops/deletes it. Short phrases receive overlapping fades. Gesture repeats primary-knob movements and can follow tap/MIDI-clock speed changes. These are different recording functions; preset-change continuity and power-cycle audio retention remain unverified.

### Microcosm — startup expression and supply-source conflicts

Checked **2026-11-05**. The [current manufacturer FAQ](https://www.hologramelectronics.com/pages/support) says EXP connected at power-on automatically targets **Filter**, commonly starting heel-down/closed and suppressing wet audio. The original manual’s retained-mapping statement therefore cannot be treated as unconditional startup behavior. Exact firmware/connection-dependent reconciliation remains unknown.

The same FAQ requires **greater than450mA** supply capability; the manual gives **400mA minimum**. Both specify9V center-negative. Preserve both source requirements; no measured draw orhardware revision inferred. FAQ Effect Volume changes wet output only, excluding dry audio; this is not universal volume.

The [manufacturer firmware page](https://www.hologramelectronics.com/pages/microcosm-firmware) lists **v1.13,May2022** as current; updates use MIDI with browser orSysEx transfer. ### Microcosm — looper concurrency and preset-storage exceptions

Checked **2026-11-05**, [manufacturer-authored manual on Zikinf](https://www.zikinf.com/manuels/hologram-electronics-microcosm-manuel-utilisateur-en-79235.pdf),25 PDF pages matching the manufacturer-linked original’s contents,controls andpagination; revision unstamped. Original endpoint was inaccessible this check.

Printed pp13–18: **Phrase Looper clears Hold Sampler material and disables Hold access**. Looper Only retains pitch modulation,reverb andfilter; it is not total processing bypass. **Burst disables overdubbing; its audio is not preset-saved unless Burst is deselected after recording**. Normal preset saving mixes overdubs into one loop file; Global Configuration is excluded. Thus the earlier loop-saving headline has mode-specific limits.

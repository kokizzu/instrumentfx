## Beebo — recovered manual and regional hardware evidence

Checked 2026-11-03. [Original Beebo/Hector manual](https://storage.googleapis.com/poly_effects1/manual.pdf), printed pp8–9,18–19, was readable in the maker’s storage bucket, also used by its official firmware links. Exact Beebo/Hector title, maker contact/license text and matching Beebo controls establish identity. No revision stamp recovered; it is older than the FAQ’s latest serial ranges. This resolves readable-manual access, not current-firmware equivalence.

| Field | Evidence / qualification |
|---|---|
| Controls | Manual: **two knobs**, right coarse/left fine for the touched slider; **three footswitches**, leftA/centerB/rightC. No separate physical-button census or visual inspection claimed. |
| Preset stepping | Manual: center+right or center+left steps through the set list. Pair direction unspecified. Startup loads the first set-list preset; not last-used recall or direct EXP-position retention. |
| Bypass / assignments | Right switch defaults to bypass; all three can map to modules, including multiple modules per switch. This is patch/module control, not global volume assignment or proof of bypass topology. |
| Conversion | Manual: **48kHz,24-bit conversion,32-bit float internally**. Not USB audio-format proof. The±5V I/O paragraph is explicitly **Hector**, so it does not establish Beebo analog expression/CV tolerance. |

[Identified Japanese regional distributor, exact Beebo page](https://fukusan.com/products/poly_effects/beebo/) separately specifies **152W×130D×67Hmm including knobs/protrusions;556g**. That rectangle fits200×140mm before cabling, **regional evidence**, not reconciliation of the maker’s152-wide/125-high wording. Both remain preserved.

Its four audio inputs/four outputs use paired **¼-inch TRS sockets IN1+2/3+4 and OUT1+2/3+4**, requiring TRS-to-two-mono splitters for individual channels. These are paired unbalanced channels, not balanced mono jacks. It lists3.5mm MIDI IN/OUT and USB flash-drive patch/update functions. Dedicated headphones and direct analog EXP are not documented in that inventory; do not infer absence from this alone. The regional power note says adapter separate,9V center-negative≥500mA,2.1/5.5mm barrel; included contents in Indonesia remain unverified.

Regional prose explicitly supports guitar-driven synthesis and drum-model/16-step sequencer construction.

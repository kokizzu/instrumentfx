## MC6 PRO

Checked2026-10-31 against the [current manufacturer user manual](https://help.morningstar.io/en/article/mc6-pro-user-manual-psevjy/), its [earlier manufacturer manual](https://manuals.morningstar.io/mc-midi-controller/mc6-pro-user-manual), and [manufacturer introduction](https://www.morningstar.io/post/mc6-pro-release).

**Six physical footswitches (A–F), three color LCD screens.** Each switch corresponds to the nearest displayed preset name; therefore this is a multi-screen controller, not six individual screens. Screen centers also show bank/page, bank name, last-pressed switch name, expression position and MIDI-clock BPM. Color-coded labels do not add physical displays. Exact screen diagonal, resolution and touch remain unverified in this pass.

MC6 PRO is a **MIDI controller**, kept outside audio multi-effects inventory/counts. Exact Indonesian hardware offers, prices, full I/O/power/software profile and other model variants remain unverified; do not transfer MC8 PRO or MC6 MkII specifications. No firmware/app download, installation or device operation.

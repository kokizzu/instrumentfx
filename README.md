## What belongs in the comparison

Guitar multi-effects, including effects-only processors, plus separate bass and genuine vocal multi-effects categories. Amp modeling and a built-in expression pedal are not mandatory. Multiple modes on a dedicated acoustic simulator, pitch shifter, vocoder or synth pedal do not automatically make it eligible. A guitar-thru or guitar-reverb side path does not turn a vocal pedal into a guitar multi-effects processor.

## Required evidence

Expression; separate knobs/footswitches/buttons; used/new IDR; blocks/category choices; I/O; factory/user memory; synth/acoustic functions; dimensions/weight; power/battery/USB operation; IR/NAM/proprietary captures; editor platforms; **screen size, monochrome/color and touchscreen**. Other live/practice features remain included.

Unknown is not No. Color or touch-control buttons do not prove a touchscreen; USB does not prove power/audio; proprietary models do not prove native NAM. Preserve firmware/revision conflicts and inaccessible-manual limitations. Seller offers support prices, not unsupported specifications.

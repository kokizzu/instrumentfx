## Scope and ownership

For guitars,include **multi-effects processors only**. Effects-only processors can qualify without amp modeling or a built-in pedal. Multiple modes on a dedicated-purpose pedal do not automatically qualify. **Flamma FV01,BOSS SY-1,SY-200,AC-3,VO-1 and XS-100 remain excluded** from active counts and candidate tables. Genuine bass and vocal categories remain separate; guitar-thru or chord detection alone is not guitar multi-effects. Adjacent capability leads such as Iridium are labelled as such and require explicit scope admission before entering active multi-effects counts.

Ever-owned coverage includes former/donated and hand-operated multi-effects. The preserved ownership overlay has21 identified entries,20 with technical profiles; the older BlackBox revision,exact Zoom vocal model/bundle and RH-4 ownership remain unresolved. AME-100 ownership does not establish AVE-100 ownership. The rejected MG-400 order and GP-50 backup plan are not confirmed purchases. Private receipt/order/payment information must not be published.

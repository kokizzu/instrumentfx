# Multi-effects comparison scope

[Comparison index](README.md) · [Owner history](OWNERSHIP.md) · [Resume](RESUME.md)

**User correction, 2026-10-14: for guitars, only multi-effects; Flamma FV01 is not to be included as a multi-effects unit.** This instruction supersedes older broad-scope wording in the initial methodology, backlog, checkpoint and historical counts.

## Included

Guitar multi-effects processors with a documented range of independent effect categories, including effects-only processors. An amp modeler, built-in expression pedal, touchscreen, a minimum number of footswitches or an FX loop is not a prerequisite. Do not exclude Line 6 HX Effects merely because it lacks amp modeling.

Retain the original separate bass and genuine vocal multi-effects categories. A vocal unit is not a guitar multi-effects processor merely because it accepts a guitar for chord detection, passes guitar audio through or adds guitar reverb. Evaluate each signal path separately.

Multi-instrument/synth processors such as the already-researched GM-800 and the GR-55 owner-history finding are considered by their documented independent effects architecture. This does not admit every dedicated synth pedal automatically.

These are project-scope decisions, not claims that a device contains only one algorithm or lacks all secondary processing. Preserve published specifications in their source-backed findings; do not alter facts to fit a label. **Multiple modes, presets or supporting reverb do not automatically establish eligibility.**

Controller-only products, tuners, ABY switches, power supplies, cases and downloadable presets remain excluded. Borderline units require a stated classification decision; do not silently count them.

## Counts and older research

Archived/excluded items must not be included in active multi-effects totals, recommendations or candidate tables. The repository's previously quoted 80-record count was a broad historical research count, not an audited count under this narrower scope. Do not repeat that number as the active multi-effects total without applying this exclusion list and checking subsequent integrations.

Findings notes and owner-history entries are not automatically new integrated comparison records. Ownership is a separate attribute: a donated unit stays in ever-owned history, and an out-of-scope pedal may be retained as history without being promoted to an active multi-effects candidate.

The FV01 rows are removed from `shopee-multifx-3.md`. `specialist.md` distinguishes the eligible GM-800 record from the five excluded archived pedals. Old dated price and firmware evidence is retained; its retention does not restore eligibility. Older backlog mentions of single-purpose pedals are inactive under this rule.

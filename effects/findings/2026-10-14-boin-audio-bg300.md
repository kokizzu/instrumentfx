## Conflicts requiring resolution

Nafiri's description [R1] says **1.77-inch TFT,70 effects,10 factory cabs,512-point IR and a9V input**, whereas its linked manual says **1.7-inch LCD**, enumerates64 factory choices/15 cabs and shows USB power without a barrel socket. The description also mentions a looper not documented in this manual. Do not infer a hardware revision, firmware update or shared BG200/400 specification to explain this without evidence. Confirm the actual unit's rear panel, firmware and matching manual before relying on these power/port details.

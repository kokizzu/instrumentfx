## Firmware and source qualifications

The dated September2026 importer announcements establish a later feature state than the still-accessible80-preset landing page. They do not establish which firmware is installed on the owner's unit. Jazz Gold/White Rose are explicitly described as finishes of the same platform, not separate technical models. An AI Tone Companion generates/adjusts settings; it is not evidence of native NAM or physical onboard capture.

The website's Bluetooth-speaker wording is broader than its detailed audio/BLE descriptions. Exact Bluetooth transmission/headphone support is therefore not independently established here. USB power compatibility is recorded as specified, not inferred from connector shape.

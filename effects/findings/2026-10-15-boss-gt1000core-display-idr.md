# BOSS GT-1000CORE — display and IDR follow-up

Checked **2026-10-15**. This updates an existing record in [BOSS comparison](../guitar-boss.md); it is **not a new model**. Earlier technical research remains dated2026-10-14 unless explicitly rechecked here.

## Display

- **One graphic LCD,256×80 pixels**, manufacturer specification. Published diagonal was not located and remains **unknown**, not estimated from the chassis.
- The [official product photograph](https://static.roland.com/products/gt-1000core/images/gt-1000core_top_gal.jpg) was successfully opened and visually inspected: **monochrome-style readout**. Colored footswitch LEDs are not a color graphical display. The manufacturer does not separately state the screen's color depth; this is visual evidence, not a panel-datasheet claim.
- **Touchscreen: unverified/no touch interface documented**. Color/monochrome appearance and knob navigation alone are not definitive touch-hardware specifications.

The [manufacturer product/specification page](https://www.boss.info/global/products/gt-1000core/) also confirms **7 knobs,3 footswitches,5 buttons**,24 simultaneous blocks,185 effect types,250 factory+250 user locations and16 IR slots. It lists system program2.02 and Tone Studio4.1.1; this is a publication observation, not the owner's installed version. Complete current category counts and synth/acoustic algorithms remain open.

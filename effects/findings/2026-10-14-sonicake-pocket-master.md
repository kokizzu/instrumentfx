# Sonicake Pocket Master QME-10 — display and IDR evidence

Research date: **2026-10-14**. Existing model; inventory unchanged. [Comparison](../guitar-compact.md) · [Resume](../RESUME.md). Status: **partial**.

## Primary technical evidence

The [manufacturer's multilingual manual for firmware 1.3.0](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0100/1950/4185/files/Pocket_Master_Multi_Languages_User_Manual_Firmware_V1.3.0.pdf?v=1754556312), English printed p.5, was visually inspected. It specifies a **1.77-inch color LCD**.

The panel has **1 rotary control and 2 hand-operated buttons, not conventional footswitches**. It also documents **two USB-C sockets with different roles**: audio/data and power/charging. The shared stereo output/headphone arrangement remains distinct from a dedicated headphone socket.

**Touchscreen: unverified.** Physical-control navigation is documented, but an explicit touch specification was not established. No screen dimension is derived from the device's housing or a scaled photograph.

The [current product page](https://www.sonicake.com/products/pocket-master) lists newer software/firmware than the cited manual. Keep the manual version alongside firmware-dependent facts instead of silently assuming every release has identical functionality.

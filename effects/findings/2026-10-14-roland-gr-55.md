## Firmware and software evidence

The manufacturer publishes a launch-era 270-guitar/90-bass preset specification alongside system version 1.50. Its [release notes][firmware] explicitly add 90 guitar and 18 bass presets. The updated 360/108 totals above are arithmetic derived from those two primary sources, not a count of the owner's installed patches. Record the firmware context rather than silently selecting one figure.

The [Windows Librarian download page][librarian] establishes software availability and Windows requirements, not a verified inventory of its editing functions. Current official driver downloads include Windows 10/11 and macOS versions; driver compatibility is not editor compatibility or proof of USB bus power.

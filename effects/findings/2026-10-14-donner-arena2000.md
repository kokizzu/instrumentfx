## Apps and evidence limitations

**Windows and macOS editing software** are explicitly linked on the [manufacturer German product page][de]. **Android editor** is established by [DONNER LLC's Arena2000 app listing][android], which describes sound-library editing and shows a July2026 update. Bluetooth mobile editing is advertised, but a primary iOS listing was not established in this pass; **iOS and Linux remain unverified** rather than borrowed from reviews.

The official product page links [EC1790_update.pdf][manual]. Retrieval failed because the file exceeded the web tool's size limit, and the alternate download attempt also failed. **The PDF was not read**; dimensions, algorithm tables and detailed power/button specifications remain open. Manufacturer gallery images were inspected directly, not measured for dimensions. Customer reviews and product-page Q&A—including unrelated speaker claims—were not used as technical evidence.

## Unresolved archival qualifications

**Power conflict:** the manual's precautions say **500 mA**, while its rear-panel drawing and printed specification page say **1.5 A**. The older indexed [manufacturer page][product] also says500mA. The conflict is inside manufacturer evidence; it is not silently resolved by calling one value measured draw and the other adapter rating. The actual unit label and supplied adapter would need checking. Generic battery warnings do not establish an internal battery compartment.

**Routing:** in individual mode, guitar goes to GUITAR OUT and vocals to XLR OUT. Mixed mode sends their mixture to both. These are different destinations, not a stereo-output pair. The guitar input is not documented as intelligent-harmony detection. [Store][shop], [manual][manual].

Power revision, dimension labels, state retention, used offers and live seller/accessory confirmation were unresolved in the original research. They are **not active multi-effects backlog items** after exclusion. Do not add unsupported features from a different FV-series pedal or re-admit FV01 because it has several supporting processing functions.

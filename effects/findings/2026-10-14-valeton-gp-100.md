# Valeton GP-100 — display, controls and IDR evidence

Research date: **2026-10-14**. Existing model, not an additional inventory entry. [Comparison](../guitar-compact.md) · [Resume](../RESUME.md). Status remains **partial**.

## Technical findings

The [manufacturer's firmware-1.8 manual, German edition hosted by Thomann](https://images.thomann.de/pics/atg/atgdata/document/manual/488027_de.pdf) establishes:

- **Color graphical display**: tuner instructions describe red/yellow/green indications; the looper illustration also contains multiple colors. **Diagonal remains unverified. Touchscreen remains unverified**: the documented editing workflow uses physical controls, but absence of a touch instruction is not treated as an explicit hardware specification.
- **4 rotary controls / 2 footswitches / 5 separate buttons**: PARA plus three quick-access rotaries; GLOBAL, DRUM, EDIT, SAVE and EXIT. The built-in expression pedal is separate. See printed pp.3–4 and 7.
- **Looper correction:** pre-effects mode allows **90 seconds mono**, while post-effects mode allows **45 seconds stereo**. The previous comparison mentioned only the pre-effects mode. See printed p.6, visually inspected.
- DC supply specification: **9 V, 500 mA, centre-negative**, printed p.1. USB-powered operation and batteries are not established by this finding.

The manufacturer manual's effect-total wording differs from the [current product page](https://www.valeton.net/product/gp-100/); this pass does not resolve that firmware/library-count difference. Do not silently overwrite a dated total.

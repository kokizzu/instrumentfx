# Hotone Ampero II Stage MP-380

Reviewed 2026-10-14. Guitar/bass/vocal multi-effects, **large**, no built-in expression. This is Stage, not MP-350 or Stomp. Partial record: unresolved effect-list, power-operation and market details remain explicit.

## Hardware, display and memory

| Field | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Expression | No built-in pedal; **2 × 6.35 mm TRS EXP/CTRL sockets**. External pedal/toe switching is not an included pedal [M,P] |
| Rotary / footswitch / buttons | **5 / 8 / 1**: volume, MENU/VALUE, three quick-access knobs; seven programmable plus one Mode switch; screen-lock button. Rear power/ground switches separate; encoder presses not counted twice [M] |
| Size/weight | **301 W × 180 D × 58 H mm; 1895 g**, hence large under the 300 mm medium-width boundary. Cable clearance additional [P] |
| Display | **5-inch, 800×480, color touchscreen**. LED strip is not a second graphical screen [P,M] |
| Memory | **300 fully editable locations = 60 banks × 5**, five scenes per patch. Factory-filled subset not established [M,P] |
| Routing/blocks | **12 simultaneous slots**, dual serial/parallel chains, DSP-limited. Product specification lists 16 module types; the older V1.0.4 manual lists 13. These are library categories, not additional simultaneous slots [M,P] |

## Processing and loading

Published library: **460+**, with 68 cabinets, 100+ modeled pedals and 60+ original effects; the same global page says both 87 and 90+ amps. Do not sum overlapping marketing counts or represent launch documentation as a fully transcribed current library. Dynamic, frequency/filter, wah, volume, drive, EQ, preamp, amp, cab, IR, modulation, delay and reverb are documented categories; current per-category subtotals remain pending [M,P].

**IR:** 50 user slots; 24-bit/44.1 kHz mono WAV; 1024/2048 samples. Twenty Celestion IRs are supplied separately [P]. **Native NAM A2 Lite:** documented with firmware **1.7.0+**; not unrestricted A2 Full loading. Earlier Sound Clone conversion and proprietary Tone Catch capture are separate capabilities [N,J]. Acoustic effects/presets are advertised, but exact electric-to-acoustic and synth algorithm names remain unverified here; vocal input and pitch effects alone do not prove synthesis.

# Sonicake Matribox QME-50 — display and regional-price warning

Research date: **2026-10-14**. Existing model, not a new inventory entry. [Comparison](../guitar-compact.md) · [Resume](../RESUME.md). Status: **partial**.

## Display and controls

The [official product photograph](https://www.sonicake.com/cdn/shop/products/QME-50.jpg?v=1678434988&width=1096) shows a **color graphical display**. **Diagonal and touchscreen capability remain unverified**; neither is measured or inferred from the photograph.

The [manufacturer manual for firmware 1.1.0](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0100/1950/4185/files/Matribox_Online_Manual_Firmware_V1.1.0.pdf?v=1763367433), printed p.3, was visually inspected: **5 rotary controls / 2 footswitches / 4 separate buttons**, plus the built-in expression pedal. This corroborates the previous count. The documented navigation is knob/button-based; that observation is not an explicit touch specification.

## Indonesian offer: do not mix regional variants

A [Jakmall listing from LOBAJET](https://www.jakmall.com/lobajet/sonicake-matribox-qme-50-multi-effects-processor-pedal) was indexed at **Rp2.095.650** and identifies the item as the **Chinese/CN-language version**, with a warning that the language cannot be changed. The listing also specifies a preorder workflow. This is **seller-reported variant information**, not independent proof that all QME-50 hardware has the same language restriction.

## Processing, memory and loading

| Model | Fixed sound stages | Choices per category | Presets | Synth / acoustic / loading |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Electric | Preamp → MOD → delay → reverb → IR; tuner separate | 9 preamps,2 MOD,1 delay,1 reverb,8 IRs | **3 factory-filled overwriteable total**, not3+3 | No synth/acoustic algorithm identified;8 replaceable IRs, exact import limits unresolved; NAM not established |
| AC | EQ → compressor → anti-feedback → MOD → SPACE → IR | EQ1,COMP1,anti-feedback1 up to8 notches,MOD2,SPACE2,IR8 | **3 factory-filled overwriteable total** | Acoustic-input/body-IR processing, not verified electric-to-acoustic modeling;8 replaceable IRs; NAM not established |
| Bass | Boost → EQ → compressor → MOD → SPACE → IR | Boost1,EQ1,COMP1,MOD2,SPACE2,IR8 | **3 editable total**; original overwrite details need confirmation | Bass cabinet IR processing; no synth/acoustic modeling documented;8 replaceable IRs; NAM not established |

**AC and Bass select delay OR reverb in their shared SPACE block.** Electric has separate delay and reverb stages. MOD differs: electric chorus/phaser, AC chorus/tremolo, Bass chorus/phaser. Cabinet selector OFF is not an extra IR choice. Counting tuner as a utility must not inflate the number of independently usable sound stages.

## Connections, power and apps

All three manuals enumerate mono6.35 mm TS input and main output, separate headphone/mobile-recording sockets and USB data/charge. Small-jack dimensions/wiring and USB subtype remain unresolved in the inspected primary text. No microphone/phantom, XLR, FX loop, physical MIDI or EXP connector is established. USB recording/playback and24-bit/48 kHz processing are documented; exact interface channels and IR import limits remain unknown.

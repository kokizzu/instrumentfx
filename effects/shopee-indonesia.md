**Checked 2026-10-14. Priority: multi-effects listed on Shopee Indonesia.** This page records actual hardware-listing discoveries and separates researched models from pending specifications. It is **not a complete inventory of every Shopee seller, a current-stock guarantee, or a recommendation ranking**. A search result or fetched listing may be older than the retrieval date.

A product URL establishes listing evidence, not authenticity, seller authorization, current availability, condition grade, exact selected variant, delivered cost or completed-sale value. Prices below are **IDR asking/listed observations**. `Sold out` is historical listing evidence, not proof that a transaction completed. A seller's name containing Official, or an Original title, is not independent authentication. No prices are converted from foreign currency.

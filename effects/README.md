## Size and expression categories

Use manufacturer unit dimensions, **W×D×H in millimetres**, not packaging.

| Bin | Width/depth test |
|---|---|
| Compact | Width≤200 mm **and** depth≤140 mm |
| Medium | Width≤300 mm and depth≤200 mm, excluding compact |
| Large | Exceeds medium width or depth |
| Unknown | Width or depth unverified |

Do not rotate a device to force a fit. Plugs, strain relief and pedal travel need additional space. Distinguish built-in expression, external-only expression, and neither/unverified; a socket is not an included pedal. Conflicting dimension labels are preserved rather than silently normalized.

## Required comparison fields

Expression; rotary/footswitch/button counts; separate used/new IDR; simultaneous blocks and category choices; I/O; factory/user memory; synth/acoustic functions; dimensions/weight; power/battery/USB operation; IR/NAM/proprietary captures; editor platforms; **screen diagonal, monochrome/color and touch**.

Extra live/practice fields include headphone/DI/phantom support, USB audio/reamp, MIDI, scenes, routing, bypass/trails, looper, rhythms, firmware and special pickup/cable requirements. [METHODOLOGY.md](METHODOLOGY.md) defines these terms.

`?` means unverified, not No. A color display does not prove touch; USB does not imply power/audio; a proprietary capture loader does not establish native NAM. A foreign-to-IDR estimate requires a sourced dated rate and explicit FX label. No asking price establishes a completed sale.

## What each record contains

Hardware/expression and K/F/B controls; screen size/color/touch; simultaneous blocks/category choices; factory/user memories; synth versus acoustic sim/amp/body IR; I/O; voltage/polarity/current/battery/USB; IR versus native/converted NAM/proprietary captures; editor/updater/audio platforms; used/new IDR evidence; additional stage/practice functions. Exact manufacturer/manual/firmware and seller links are in each finding.

`?` means unverified, not No. A color LCD is not necessarily a touchscreen. An expression socket is not an included pedal. USB data/audio does not establish USB power. Importing IRs does not establish amp capture or native NAM. A manual revision is not an independent test of current hardware firmware.

**Size bins:** compact width≤200mm AND depth≤140mm;medium≤300×200 excluding compact;large otherwise;unknown if dimensions/axes are unverified. Do not rotate to pass a fit test. Plugs and pedal travel need extra room.

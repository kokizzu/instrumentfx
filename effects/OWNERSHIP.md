## Evidence and privacy

Ownership evidence comes from the owner's gear statements, especially the explicit 2026-07-08 list and the 2026-10-12/13 corrections, plus purchase/delivery notifications inspected in the owner's connected mailbox. Only necessary model-level conclusions are published. Private messages, mailbox links/IDs, order numbers, addresses, payment information and receipt contents are not copied into this public repository. Receipt dates are delivery-notification dates, not silently relabelled order dates. Technical specifications require separate manufacturer evidence; a seller's receipt title is not a specification sheet.

A delivery notification is purchase/delivery evidence, not independent authentication, proof of current possession or proof of no later return. Explicit owner corrections override misleading order titles. An additional broad mailbox search was rate-limited during this review; unresolved items remain visible rather than guessed.

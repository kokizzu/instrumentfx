All primary price columns now use **IDR**. The [unchanged original comparison](guitar-boss.history-2026-10-14.md) preserves every former USD quote, source and technical statement as historical provenance. No foreign price was relabelled or converted using an assumed rate.

`?` means unverified, not No. K/F/B = rotary controls / main footswitches / separate buttons; push-encoders counted once, toe and rear switches separate. Dimensions are **W×D×H in millimetres**, excluding plugs and cable clearance. All records remain technically partial.

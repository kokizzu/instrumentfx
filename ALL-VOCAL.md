# All vocal and live mic-path multi-effects — comparison

[Guitar](ALL-GUITAR.md) · [Bass](ALL-BASS.md) · [Vocal](ALL-VOCAL.md) · [Category](MULTI-EFFECTS.md) · [Repository](README.md)

All 17 recorded vocal or live mic-path multi-effects: 9 vocal-only and 8 with independent acoustic/general-instrument processing. Guitar-thru/chord detection is not a processed guitar path. FV01 remains excluded in [SINGLE-VOCAL.md](SINGLE-VOCAL.md); a mic input does not imply harmony or pitch correction.

These are **comparisons of the recorded catalogue**, not exhaustive market coverage or fresh stock certification. Organized 2026-10-16 from the linked source records; their original technical/price dates, firmware, revision and access limits still apply. Every model remains partial. **? means unverified, not No.** Read the complete linked record for sources and conflicting evidence; this view does not silently resolve those conflicts.

K/F/B = independently adjustable rotary controls / footswitches / separate buttons; toe and power switches are additional unless explicitly stated. Body axes and mass qualifications are retained. Color does not prove touch; USB audio does not prove bus power; IRs, native NAM, converted NAM and proprietary captures differ.

# NUX multi-effects

[All guitar](ALL-GUITAR.md) · [All bass](ALL-BASS.md) · [Category](MULTI-EFFECTS.md) · [Repository](README.md)

**Ten active profiles:** nine previously researched MG/TRIDENT models consolidated below, plus **Cerberus NME-3 newly researched2026-10-15**. Older records retain their original observation dates. Every record remains partial. MG-300 and MG-300 MKII are different hardware; **MG-30 GEN2 is a firmware generation**,not an automatically separate hardware model. Controller-only products,power supplies and single-purpose stompboxes are not added simply because NUX sells them.

`?` means unverified,not No. K/F/B = rotary/main footswitch/separate front button counts; encoder presses count once,toe and rear switches separate. Dimensions are unit dimensions,not packaging. NUX sometimes labels the long horizontal dimension L; normalized W/D and any uncertainty are explicitly noted. Price columns use IDR; original foreign observations are preserved separately without guessed conversion.

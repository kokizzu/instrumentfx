## Inclusion, ownership and migration limits

A guitar input used only for harmony detection,through output or added guitar reverb does not automatically make a vocal device a guitar multi-effects processor. VE-8 and Play Acoustic are retained for their documented acoustic-guitar processing,not treated as interchangeable electric-amp modelers. Dedicated SY-1,SY-200,AC-3,VO-1,XS-100 and FV01 remain excluded.

Ever-owned status is independent of current Shopee availability and current possession. Do not turn the rejected MG-400 order or GP-50 shopping plan into an ownership tag. Former/donated gear remains; private receipt/order information stays out of the repository.

The root organization is **not yet fully migrated**: legacy links above expose records still awaiting complete brand consolidation. All97 records are partial; an index entry is not proof of a current seller offer or complete technical verification. Resume and exact commit/inventory state live in root `research-state.json`. Do not create GitHub workflows or further progress Markdown files.

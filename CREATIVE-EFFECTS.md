# Synth, pitch, instrument simulation and special effects

[Repository](README.md) · [Guitar comparison](all-guitar.md) · [Bass comparison](all-bass.md) · [Vocal comparison](all-vocal.md)

This category compares the effects themselves: synthesis, harmony/transposition, instrument sounds and unusual processing. Amp/cab model totals and NAM compatibility do not establish these capabilities.

## Reading the comparison

- **Synth:** distinguish an oscillator or instrument-tone engine from resonant filters, octave-plus-rotary recipes and free-running generators.
- **Pitch:** polyphony belongs to a particular algorithm. Stereo audio and multiple generated voices do not prove chord tracking.
- **Instrument simulation:** acoustic/sitar/fretless processing, piano/organ tones generated by a synthesizer, and acoustic IRs are different capabilities.
- **Special:** examples include freeze, slicer, glitch, granular processing, bit reduction, ring modulation, sequenced filters and feedback simulation. This is a practical grouping, not a standardized manufacturer category.
- **Unverified** means the checked evidence did not establish a feature; it does not prove absence.

## Limits that change the choice

- **[Line 6 HX Effects](brand/LINE6.md#hx-effects):** Nine blocks overall, DSP permitting. [Snapshots retain block topology](brand/LINE6.md#hx-effects--snapshot-topology-and-shared-switch-states); alternative models must already fit in that preset. The 3.80 owner’s manual restricts the Feedbacker/poly group to **one per path**;do not rewrite that as one per preset. A [separate HX Edit3.80 instance table](brand/LINE6.md#hx-effects--editor-and-owner-guide-instance-limit-conflicts) instead says one per preset;the source conflict remains unresolved,andtwo simultaneous heavy models are not guaranteed.
- **[BOSS GT-1000CORE](brand/BOSS.md#gt-1000core):** FX1–FX3, PEDAL FX and MASTER DELAY have different roles. Harmonist, Pedal Bend and Sound Hold require single notes; only OCTAVE POLY explicitly establishes polyphonic octave-down.
- **[Eventide H90](brand/EVENTIDE.md#h90):** Two algorithms per Program. [Synth envelope/filter distinctions](brand/EVENTIDE.md#h90--synth-envelope-and-filter-control-distinctions) retain separate Gate, Freeze Release and Sweep B roles. HotSawz’s six oscillators do not provide chord tracking; granular quartet requires 1.12.5. Installed firmware and tracking performance untested. [Freeze/flex limits](brand/EVENTIDE.md#h90--algorithm-specific-freeze-and-flex-behavior): PolyFlex Auto Engage affects pad audibility; Prism Shift leaves its middle voice live. [Granular capture/trigger limits](brand/EVENTIDE.md#h90--granular-trigger-capture-and-dry-path-distinctions) distinguish reverb freeze, voice repeats, dry interruption and grain modulation.
- **[Original MOOER GE300](brand/MOOER.md#ge300):** Original GE300 only. Synth tracking stays first; FXA mono and FXB poly pitch differ. [Touch/LFO filtering and arpeggiator speeds](brand/MOOER.md#ge300--synth-topology-and-expression-revision-limits-2026-10-28) have distinct controls and units. Original manual: Detune±100 cents; Lofi1.5–44.1kHz/1–16bit controls. Harmony requires 3.0.1 and single-note playing; NAM conversion into AMP does not expand the synth engine.
- **[Zoom B1on](brand/ZOOM.md#b1on):** Ba Synth and 4VoiceSyn explicitly require single notes; other synth chord support is unverified. Some effects require 1.2; B1Xon pedal-pitch algorithms are excluded.
- **[BOSS GM-800](brand/BOSS.md#gm-800):** Requires compatible divided-pickup/Serial GK for guitar/bass synthesis; MIDI triggering is separate. Firmware-qualified normal-pickup routing and [separate inter-device stereo GK Audio](brand/BOSS.md#gm-800--gk-audio-is-a-separate-serial-stereo-path) exist, but no direct analog guitar input. Audio MFX polyphony and normal-pickup/GK Audio effects coverage remain unverified.
- **[NUX MG-300 MKII](brand/NUX.md#mg-300-mkii):** Harmonist/Detune illustration leads could not be independently viewed with available tools; their input polyphony/ranges stay unverified. Block substitution does not create additional positions. Pitch Bender is not borrowed from original MG-300.

The tables describe available algorithms, not a claim that every listed effect runs simultaneously. Dedicated single-purpose pedals remain in the [separate guitar archive](single-guitar.md); they do not increase active multi-effects counts.

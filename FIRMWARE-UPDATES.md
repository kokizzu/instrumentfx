## Reading the ranking

Duration is the device-firmware release date minus the first hardware release date, using the stated announcement or shipping basis. Editors, drivers, updater apps, manual revisions and web crawl dates are not device-firmware releases. Beta firmware is identified in the model evidence.

Exact elapsed days are divided by 365.2425. Month/year precision produces minimum–maximum bounds. Rows sort by the largest lower bound, then upper bound; overlapping intervals do not establish a strict order. Negative or overlapping release dates remain visible. The span is an observation, not continuous support, update frequency or a promised lifetime. Unknown, unavailable and documented non-updatable are distinct.

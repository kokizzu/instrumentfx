### Category counts and firmware context

The1.8.0 appendix separates all eleven categories. Individually transcribed complete groups: **NR3,EQ6,Reverb12,Volume1**; AMP and DST also each contain **five SnapTone import locations**, not five extra factory amp algorithms. Complete PRE/WAH/DST/AMP/CAB/MOD/DLY subtotals remain untranscribed. The advertised240+ and140+ amp/cab plus100+ pedal figures overlap and are not summed into a definitive current grand total. [M]

SnapTone support is model-specific evidence from the new manual, not an inference from newer GP-150/180 hardware. The2026-08-18 regional notice explicitly includes GP-200LT in the GP-200-family A2 conversion list. [N]

### Ports, recording and practical operation

One6.35 mm mono instrument input; two6.35 mm main outputs accepting **TRS balanced or TS unbalanced connections**; mono TS FX send/return;3.5 mm stereo aux and dedicated headphones; separate3.5 mm TRS MIDI IN/OUT-THRU; two external EXP/FS;USB-C andDC. **There are two main output sockets, not separate pairs for the manual's balanced and unbalanced specification lines.** No XLR microphone/phantom connection in the complete inventory. [M pp.4,78]

The FX-loop insertion follows the AMP module as it is moved. USB normal mode offers **6-in/4-out with MIDI**; simplified mobile mode offers stereo USB audio **without MIDI/editor connection**. Recording supports16/24-bit44.1kHz,with dry/wet,reamp,loopback and optional AUX-to-USB routing. Independent headphone volume is documented. [P,M pp.4,13–14,25,78]

**180-second looper,100 rhythms**, tuner,bypass/mute,assignable switches,globalEQ and delay/reverb trails. The manual's tuner-range statement may predate later update changes; no updated numerical range is asserted here. [M]

### GP-200JR — I/O, routing and practical features

Rear diagram: mono 6.35 mm TS instrument input; TS send/return; stereo 3.5 mm aux; two balanced **XLR** outputs with ground lift; two unbalanced 6.35 mm TS outputs; stereo 3.5 mm headphones; one TRS EXP/FS; USB-C and DC. No separate microphone input, phantom-power facility or physical MIDI DIN/TRS jack shown. **Do not replace the XLR pair with TRS outputs based on erroneous manual prose.** USB MIDI is separately documented. [JR-M p.4]

Normal USB mode: 6-in/4-out with MIDI and editor; legacy mobile mode: 2×2 without MIDI/editor. 16/24-bit, 44.1 kHz recording; reamp, loopback and AUX-to-USB. Input impedance modes: acoustic 4.7 MΩ, electric/bass 1 MΩ, line 10 kΩ. Movable FX-loop send/return nodes, series/parallel loop setting, configurable switch actions and global EQ; global-EQ text wrongly names LT. These qualifications are not a claim of a general dual processing path. [JR-M pp.11,13–18,75]

Looper maximum 180 seconds **without undo/redo**; post placement halves recording time. Reverse/half speed, auto recording and drum sync; 100 rhythms. Delay/reverb trails and tuner available; separate scene count not established. [JR-P, JR-M pp.6–8]
